Core Training Curriculum

Module 1

The Pharmacology of Lived Experience

Examines the divergent temporal clocks of methadone: maintenance coverage suppresses withdrawal for 24 to 36 hours, while analgesia ceases after 6 to 8 hours.

Module 2

Dismantling the Single Story

Deconstructs how reflexive punitive labeling renders chronic pain invisible. Counselors practice embracing clinical ambiguity rather than resorting to suspicion.

Module 3

Congenital Disability & Lifelong Pain

Prepares clinicians for the complex presentations of adults with spina bifida, teaching differential diagnosis between nociceptive joint strain and neuropathic lesions.

Module 4

Peer Mentorship & Communitas

Engages people with lived experience of both chronic pain and opioid recovery as peer mentors, guiding the community from liminality toward communitas through mutual accountability.

Module 5

Trauma, Culture & Cumulative Harm

Applies trauma-informed principles to individuals carrying cumulative, historical, and colonial trauma, fostering consultation models that reject paternalism.

Module 6

Community Accountability & Repair

Practices restorative responses to harm within groups, replacing punitive measures with relational repair while preserving individual dignity.

Module 7

Ethics, Scope & Reflexive Practice

Clarifies professional boundaries, referral pathways, and methods for examining personal power, bias, and scope limitations.

If someone is in danger

Peer circles and this protocol are not emergency services. In the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 for a medical emergency. SAMHSA's National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7). Carry naloxone where opioids are present and learn how to use it. Outside the US, use your local emergency number.
