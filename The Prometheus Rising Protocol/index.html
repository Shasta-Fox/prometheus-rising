Restorative Harm Reduction & Counselor Education

The Prometheus Rising Protocol

A clinical and community education program connecting molecular pharmacology to the lived realities of recovery and chronic physical pain. It prepares counselors and peer mentors to support people managing opioid dependence, and it keeps accountability in the community instead of handing it to punishment.

In Memoriam: Dedicated to Jonathan M. Martin (1984–2024). Honoring lived wisdom and mutual aid.

The Prometheus Group Online Sanctuary

Digital harm reduction spaces bring theoretical maps into direct contact with human territory. People connect through drop-in voice rooms (VC), check-ins, and peer accountability circles. Participants may keep their identities private and are invited to play, which is part of how the group learns.

Core Training Curriculum

Module 1

The Pharmacology of Lived Experience

Examines the divergent clocks of methadone: maintenance coverage suppresses withdrawal for 24 to 36 hours, while analgesia ceases after 6 to 8 hours.

Module 2

Dismantling the Single Story

Deconstructs how reflexive "drug-seeking" labels render chronic pain invisible. Counselors practice embracing clinical ambiguity instead of resolving it through suspicion of self-reports.

Module 3

Congenital Disability & Lifelong Pain

Prepares clinicians for the complex presentations of adults with spina bifida. Teaches the differential diagnosis of nociceptive joint strain versus neuropathic lesions.

Module 4

Peer Mentorship & Communitas

Engages people with lived experience of both chronic pain and opioid recovery as peer mentors, moving the community from liminality toward communitas through mutual accountability.

Module 5

Trauma, Culture & Cumulative Harm

Applies the six trauma-informed principles to people carrying cumulative, historical, and colonial trauma, and examines consultation that avoids paternalism.

Module 6

Community Accountability & Repair

Practices restorative responses to harm in online and in-person groups, replacing punishment with repair, and keeps the person's dignity in the room.

Module 7

Ethics, Scope & Reflexive Practice

Clarifies what counselors and peers do and do not do, how to refer, and how to examine one's own power, bias, and limits.

If someone is in danger

Peer circles and this protocol are not emergency services. In the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911 for a medical emergency. SAMHSA's National Helpline is 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7). Carry naloxone where opioids are present and learn how to use it. Outside the US, use your local emergency number.
